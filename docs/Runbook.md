# Runbook

For the property manager. No terminal, no code.

**Back office:** https://backoffice-resident-ai.vercel.app

## Daily

**Open the queue.** Tickets arrive as residents report them, including
overnight. Emergencies are marked and sit at the top.

**Work "needs attention" first.** That filter covers emergencies, tickets the
agent was unsure about, and anything a resident has disputed.

**Open a ticket to see everything.** The facts, the photo, and the full
conversation. Every field is editable — if a resident said "upstairs bathroom"
and you know that means Flat 8, correct it. Your edits are recorded.

**Close it when the work is done.** Only you can close a ticket; the system
cannot. Closing records who closed it and when.

## Weekly

**Check the review queue.** Anything the agent could not handle confidently.
Usually empty. Each row says why in plain language — resolve it or turn it into
a ticket.

**Add to the knowledge base.** If residents keep asking something, add the
question and answer. The agent starts answering it immediately and stops
creating tickets for it. This is your main lever over what the system does.

## What the system will never do

- Close a ticket. That is always you.
- Quote a price, a date, or a rent figure.
- Book a viewing or handle a payment.
- Send a contractor. It produces the ticket; dispatch is yours.

## What to escalate to a developer

- No tickets at all for more than an hour during the working day
- The same error appearing on every message
- A resident's number linked to the wrong person
- Photos not loading in the back office

## Emergencies

The agent recognises floods, gas smells, no heating in winter, and doors that
will not lock, and marks them as emergencies without completing the full
interview. It gives the resident immediate safety advice but never promises
that anyone is on the way. **Acting on an emergency ticket is a human
responsibility and the system does not page anyone.** If out-of-hours cover
matters, keep the existing emergency line running alongside this.
